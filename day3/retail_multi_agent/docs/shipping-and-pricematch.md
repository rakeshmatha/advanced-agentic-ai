# Shipping, Pickup, and Price Match

## Shipping

Free 2-day shipping applies to orders over $35. Orders under $35 are charged a
flat $6.99 shipping fee. Same-day delivery is available in select metro areas.

## Curbside pickup

Free curbside pickup is available at most stores. Orders placed before 6pm are
usually ready the same day. The customer receives a notification when the order is
ready.

## Price match

Walmart matches the current online price on identical, in-stock items from
Amazon, Target, and other major retailers. Marketplace third-party sellers and
clearance prices are excluded. Bring the competitor listing to a store associate
or submit it in the app.
