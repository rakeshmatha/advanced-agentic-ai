# Returns and Refunds Policy

## Standard returns

Most items can be returned within 90 days of purchase with a receipt. Refunds are
issued to the original payment method after the item is inspected.

## Electronics

Electronics (TVs, laptops, phones, tablets) must be returned within 15 days of
purchase and include all original packaging and accessories.

## Fresh grocery

Fresh grocery items are covered by the Fresh Guarantee: a full refund is issued on
any fresh item the customer is not satisfied with, no return of the item required.

## Non-returnable items

Gift cards, prescription medications, and clearance "final sale" items cannot be
returned.
