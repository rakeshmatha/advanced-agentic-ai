# Customer Service Policy

## Returns

Customers can request a return within 30 days of delivery for an unopened item.
The item must be in its original packaging. Refunds are issued to the original
payment method after the returned item is inspected.

## Damaged Items

Customers should contact support within seven days of delivery for an item that
arrives damaged. Include the order number and a photo of the damage so support
can arrange the next step.

## Shipping

Standard shipping usually takes three to five business days after an order ships.
A tracking link is sent by email when the carrier receives the package.

## Human Support

When a question is not covered by this policy, the assistant should explain that
it does not have enough information and recommend contacting a human support
representative.
